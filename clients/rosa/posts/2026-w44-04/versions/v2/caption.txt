Uluwatu, hour by hour. rosa.surf/spot/uluwatu, it's free.

Every 3 hours: surf height, swell period and direction, wind and whether it's offshore, cross or onshore. The best session gets its own tag, the tide gets its own curve, first light gets its own line.
