# Bento rating bars

Hook: Good. But why Good?

Tiles snap together into a bento grid on a deep blue field: a big tile with spot-pipeline.png, then smaller tiles that pop in with Size, Period and Wind words, each tile flashing its rating colour. The grid collapses into one tile reading "Good, and here's why". The phone grows out of that tile to full screen and tilts toward camera. End card: logo, white "Every rating has a reason.", space, glowing blue button "rosa.surf".
