# App blows apart

Hook: Here's what's inside Rosa

The phone with home.png floats centre, then its layers fly apart in 3D: the cards, the bars, the map, each hanging in space over Pixabay surf footage. Big white words land between the layers: "Fast.", "Free.", "Worldwide.". The layers snap back together and the phone zooms past camera. End card: logo, white "All of it, free.", space, glowing blue button "rosa.surf".
