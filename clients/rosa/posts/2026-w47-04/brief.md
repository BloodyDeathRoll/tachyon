# No paywall, ever

Hook: No paywall. No account.

Big white words "No paywall" slam onto a dark ocean clip from Pixabay, then shatter into Rosa particles that rise and fade. The dark home screen rises in a phone that fills most of the frame and one thumb flow plays: search, tap a spot, the forecast opens, no sign-up anywhere. Each step gets a single punchy word that kicks in beside the phone and clears. Closing card: logo, white two-line text, space, glowing blue button.
