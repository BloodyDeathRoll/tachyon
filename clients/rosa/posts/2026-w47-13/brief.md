# Flat to Epic

Hook: Flat, Poor, Fair, Good, Epic

The five ratings, Flat, Poor, Fair, Good and Epic, fire across the screen as big pills in the app's colours, one per beat. The last one, Epic, zooms into the light-theme Supertubos page in a big phone, the rating pill sitting where it was. The bars fill to show what made it. Rosa particles rise in the empty space, then the closing card.
