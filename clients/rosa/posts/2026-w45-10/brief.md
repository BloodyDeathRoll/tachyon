# Swell as particles

Hook: See the swell before it lands

Thousands of blue particles stream across a black frame like a swell train. They pour into the phone screen and settle into charts.png, the swell colours lighting up. The phone rotates in 3D, the particles keep flowing past it toward a pin on the coast. End card: logo, white "See it coming.", space, glowing blue button "rosa.surf/charts".
