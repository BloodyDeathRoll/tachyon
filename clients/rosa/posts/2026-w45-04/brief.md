# Swell map flyover

Hook: Fly over this week's swell

The camera flies low over charts.png laid flat in 3D like a coastline, swell colours rolling under it. It banks up to reveal the whole phone standing on the map, then dives to map.png and lands on one spot card. Pixabay rolling swell plays behind the tilted phone. End card: logo, white "Watch the swell roll in.", space, glowing blue button "rosa.surf/charts".
