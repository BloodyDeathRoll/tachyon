# Supertubos 16 days

Hook: Supertubos, 16 days out

A fresh Pixabay barrel clip fills the screen, then the light-theme Supertubos page drops in, phone tilted in 3D and big, the clip still rolling behind it. The page scrolls day by day through the next 16 days in one smooth eased swipe while the bottom nav stays fixed. Each day's rating pill pops out of the screen for a beat and slides back in. Rosa particles rise in the empty bottom, closing card with the glowing button.
