# Worth the drive

Hook: Is it worth the drive?

A fresh Pixabay clip of a car on a coast road at sunrise pans across the screen and freezes. The dark Supertubos page punches in big over it and scrolls to tomorrow morning, bottom nav fixed in place. The rating pill turns green and the word "Go" pops out of it in huge white type. Rosa particles rise in the free space, then the closing card.
