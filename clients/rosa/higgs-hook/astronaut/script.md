# The astronaut at Punta de Lobos

Source of truth: Notion, https://app.notion.com/p/409fdf788e46811484e8cbe22544e226

**Idea.** An astronaut in a full white spacesuit floats inside a space station at a big window, looking out at Earth, and waxes the window like a surfboard, slowly: a matte, bumpy layer of surf wax builds up on the glass. Later he stands on top of the station and surfs thin air, hands swishing, making the noises himself.
Spot A: Punta de Lobos (Chile) · Spot B: El Gringo (Chile)
