# Rosa — strategy

Status: **approved at CP1, 2026-10-24.** Edits after this date are logged in CHANGELOG.md.

## The tension

Checking the surf should be free, fast, and should tell you *why* a day rates. Rosa does
that for 3,056 spots, 16 days out, four times a day. Nobody knows it exists.

## The play

Short vertical videos where the real app does one thing in 15–30 seconds on a real
spot, voiced by an avid surfer who loves it. No stock, no competitors named, ever. Every
reel ends with one ask that lands on rosa.surf. The account is the product demo nobody asked for and everybody
understands in two seconds.

## Positioning

For surfers who check the forecast every day, Rosa is the free worldwide surf forecast
that shows why it rates a day. Our advantages and values only; no comparisons.

## Pillars

| # | Pillar | What a reel shows | Proof from product.md |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Why this rating | the Size / Period / Wind bars on a real spot, "wind can only polish" | rating section |
| 2 | Swell map | `/charts` animating a swell across a coast, then one spot's week | charts route |
| 3 | Free, and why | the whole app, no account needed, open data credited on screen | pricing facts, attribution line |
| 4 | Spot spotlight | one of 3,056 spots per region, the forecast for the next 16 days | spot count, horizon |
| 5 | Swell alert moment | the push arrives, the spot is Good, the drive is worth it | push feature |
| 6 | For shops and schools | the widget on a shop's site, dark and light | widget URL |

Cadence: 3–5 reels a week (data: the biggest reach gain per post is going from 1–2 to
3–5). Mix per `/ig-plan`: proof 1, teach 1–2, opinion 1, story or offer alternating.

## Experiments, first four weeks

1. Hook formula: claim vs number-first. 2. Length: 15 s vs 30 s. 3. Cover: UI frame
vs text card. 4. CTA form: link in bio vs first comment vs URL on screen. One variable a week,
pre-registered in `calendar.yaml`.

## Kill criteria

After 8 weeks: if median sends/reach < 0.3 % **and** GA4 sessions from Instagram
< 20 / week, stop the current play and re-plan at CP1 before spending another week.

## Red lines

Operator added none (2026-10-24). These come from Rosa's own repo and stay: no
accuracy claims, no competitor names at all, no cams we do not have rights to,
attribution line on every forecast shown, no Play Store mention until public, no swim
claims. Open-Meteo non-commercial terms: no paid offer without a licence check. From
the @lineup.experience comment section: do not expose quiet or local-only spots; feature
spots that are already known.

## Posting window (Tachyon's call, 2026-10-24)

The audience is worldwide, so two rotating UTC slots: **06:30 UTC** (Europe and Africa
morning, Australia and Japan evening) and **18:30 UTC** (Americas midday to evening,
Europe evening). Slot time is a tracked feature; after four weeks `online_followers`
and the per-slot numbers decide, not this default.

## Out of scope, phase 1

TikTok, Shorts, paid ads, faces on camera, carousels (Reels only until the loop has
run four weeks).
