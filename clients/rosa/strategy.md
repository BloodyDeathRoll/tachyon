# Rosa — strategy

Status: **draft for CP1**, 2026-10-24. Operator approves or edits this file before any
calendar is written.

## The tension

Surf forecasts are either paywalled or bloated, and none of them say *why* a day is a
7. Rosa is free, fast, worldwide, and shows size, period and wind for every rating.
Nobody knows it exists.

## The play

Short vertical videos where the real app does one thing in 15–30 seconds on a real
spot, with a line a surfer would say to a friend. No faces in phase 1, no stock. Every
reel ends with one ask. The account is the product demo nobody asked for and everybody
understands in two seconds.

## Positioning

For surfers who check the forecast every day, Rosa is the free worldwide surf forecast
that shows why it rates a day, unlike paywalled apps that just give a number.

## Pillars

| # | Pillar | What a reel shows | Proof from product.md |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Why this rating | the Size / Period / Wind bars on a real spot, "wind can only polish" | rating section |
| 2 | Swell map | `/charts` animating a swell across a coast, then one spot's week | charts route |
| 3 | Free, no paywall | the app doing something a paid app charges for; never name a competitor | pricing facts, attribution line |
| 4 | Spot spotlight | one of 3,056 spots per region, the forecast for the next 16 days | spot count, horizon |
| 5 | Swell alert moment | the push arrives, the spot is Good, the drive is worth it | push feature |
| 6 | For shops and schools | the widget on a shop's site, dark and light | widget URL |

Cadence: 3–5 reels a week (data: the biggest reach gain per post is going from 1–2 to
3–5). Mix per `/ig-plan`: proof 1, teach 1–2, opinion 1, story or offer alternating.

## KPIs (`[assumed]` targets, 8-week horizon)

| KPI | Source | Target by week 8 |
|---|---|---|
| Non-follower reach share | insights | ≥ 60 % |
| Sends / reach | insights | ≥ 1 % median |
| Hold (avg watch / duration) | insights | ≥ 50 % on 15 s clips |
| Profile link taps → sessions | insights + GA4 UTM | ≥ 100 sessions / week |
| `app_install` + `push_optin_start` | GA4 | ≥ 30 / week |
| `favorite_add` (lead) | GA4 | ≥ 15 / week |

## Experiments, first four weeks

1. Hook formula: claim vs number-first. 2. Length: 15 s vs 30 s. 3. Cover: UI frame
vs text card. 4. CTA: `SWELL` keyword vs "install it". One variable a week,
pre-registered in `calendar.yaml`.

## Kill criteria

After 8 weeks: if median sends/reach < 0.3 % **and** GA4 sessions from Instagram
< 20 / week, stop the current play and re-plan at CP1 before spending another week.

## Red lines

From `product.md`: no accuracy claims, no competitor data or names in comparisons,
no cams we do not have rights to, attribution line on every forecast shown, no Play
Store mention, no swim claims. Open-Meteo non-commercial terms: no paid offer without
a licence check.

## Out of scope, phase 1

TikTok, Shorts, paid ads, faces on camera, carousels (Reels only until the loop has
run four weeks).
