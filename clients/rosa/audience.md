## What the data says today

- Spots are weighted to USA, Brazil, Australia, UK, Spain, Japan (product.md). Traffic

- Real sign-ups are near zero. Nobody is waiting for Rosa; every viewer is cold.

## Personas

**1. The everyday surfer who checks before dawn.** Surfs 1–3 times a week near home,
checks a forecast app the evening before and again at 6 am. Pays for, or tolerates
ads on, an app they do not love. Trigger state: a swell is coming and the app says
"Good" with no reason. What they want: a fast answer and the *why*. Language: "is it
worth the drive", "onshore by nine", "what's the period".

**2. The traveller planning a strike.** Picks a week off around a swell window, needs
a 10–16 day horizon and spots in places their usual app covers thinly (Brazil, Chile,
Japan, Indonesia). Trigger state: comparing three forecasts for a destination. What
they want: one worldwide app that is free and does not nag. Language: "swell window",
"two weeks out", "which coast".

**3. The surf shop, school or coastal café.** Wants live conditions on their own
site or screen without paying for an API. Trigger state: customers asking "how is it
today". What they want: an embed that looks good and credits nobody but the data.
Language: "widget", "embed", "today's conditions". This persona is a partner, not a
viewer: `collaborators` posts and the `WIDGET` keyword serve them.

`[assumed]` Swimmers and open-water people are a later audience once swim weights are
tuned; not a phase-1 persona.

## Language pack

Use their words: forecast, swell, period, offshore, onshore, glassy, blown out,
window, dawn patrol, spot, break, line-up. Avoid ours: platform, solution, insights,
experience, users.
